Who we are

A Trusted Link in the Global Healthcare Chain

Al Qabas Group was established in Muscat in 2012. In 2018 the group extended its activities into the pharmaceutical sector with the launch of Al Qabas Pharmacy L.L.C, a wholesale distributor licensed by the Ministry of Health. Since then the company has built a reliable supply network linking international manufacturers with hospitals, pharmacies and government health bodies across the Middle East and Africa. Led by a management team with more than 30 years of combined industry experience, Al Qabas Pharmacy continues to grow on the principles it was founded on: quality, compliance and long term partnership.

Where Others Stop, We Deliver
